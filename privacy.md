# Wizzy Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 24 October 2026

Wizzy is an iPhone app that tells you about the landmarks you walk past. This policy explains what information Wizzy uses, why, where it is kept, and the choices you have.

## The short version

- Your location stays on your phone. Wizzy never uploads your coordinates; when you have an account, it only saves *which landmark* you visited.
- You can use the map, stories and walks without an account.
- If you create an account, we store your email, the name you choose, your interests, the landmarks you visited, your badges and your groups. That data is stored in Stockholm, Sweden.
- No ads, no tracking, no analytics, and we never sell your data.
- You can delete your account and its data at any time from inside the app.

## What we collect and why

### Without an account

Wizzy works without an account. In that case nothing about you is sent to our servers. Everything below under "Stored only on your phone" still applies.

### If you create an account

When you sign up and use Wizzy while signed in, we store the following on our server:

| Information | Why we need it |
|---|---|
| Email address and password | To create and secure your account, sign you in, and reset your password. Passwords are stored only in hashed form; we cannot read them. |
| Display name and interests | To personalise walks and to show your name to people in your groups. |
| Landmarks you visited (which landmark, when, and whether you were pinged or marked it yourself) | To keep your progress and badges across devices. |
| Badges you earned | To show your achievements. |
| Groups you create or join (group name, city, invite code, members, and an optional iCloud Shared Album link) | To let you plan walks with friends. |

The legal basis for this is that it is necessary to provide the service you asked for (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)).

### What other people can see

If you join a group, the other members of that group can see your display name, your interests and which landmarks you have visited. Nobody outside your groups can see this information. Group members never see your email address or your location.

### Stored only on your phone

Some information never leaves your device and is not sent to us:

- **Your location.** Wizzy uses your location to show nearby landmarks, plan walks from where you are, and notify you when you pass a landmark. With "Pings" turned on and "Always" location allowed, iOS can wake Wizzy in the background to send those notifications. Your coordinates are processed on your phone only. You control this through the iOS location permission and can change it at any time in Settings.
- **Your profile photo**, if you add one.
- **Your walk journal**, planned walks and app settings.

### Photos

Wizzy can look up the iCloud Shared Albums you already have, so a group can save a link to one. If you allow photo access, Wizzy reads only the names of your shared albums and how many items they contain. It does not upload, copy or analyse your photos.

### Notifications

Landmark notifications are created on your phone. Wizzy does not use a push notification server.

### Security and service logs

To keep the service secure and working, our hosting provider automatically records technical information when the app talks to the server, such as IP address, time of the request and error messages. We also briefly record failed attempts to join a group with a wrong invite code, to block guessing; these records are deleted after one day. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in protecting the service (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)).

## Who we share data with

We do not sell your data and do not share it for advertising. We use a small number of service providers that process data on our behalf:

- **Supabase** (Supabase Inc.) hosts our database and account system. Your account data is stored in Amazon Web Services' Stockholm region in Sweden. Supabase is a US company; where its staff may access data from outside the EU (for example for support), this is covered by the EU Standard Contractual Clauses in Supabase's data processing agreement.
- **Our email delivery provider** sends account emails, such as password reset messages, to your address.
- **Apple.** When Wizzy draws walking directions, it sends the start and end points of each route to Apple Maps. Apple's handling of that information is described in Apple's privacy policy. Apple also provides the App Store and iOS features such as location services.

We may disclose information if required by law.

## How long we keep it

- **Account data** is kept until you delete your account.
- **When you delete your account** in the app (Profile → Delete account), your account, profile, visits and badges are deleted from our database straight away, and you are removed from your groups. Groups you created pass to another member, or are deleted if you were the only member. Copies may remain in our hosting provider's encrypted backups for a short period until those backups are overwritten.
- **Technical logs** are kept for a limited period by our hosting provider and then deleted.
- **Data on your phone** is removed when you delete the app.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

- access the personal data we hold about you and get a copy of it;
- correct inaccurate data;
- have your data deleted (you can do this yourself in the app at any time);
- restrict or object to certain processing;
- receive your data in a portable format;
- withdraw consent you have given, such as location or photo access, at any time in iOS Settings.

To use any of these rights, email **[wizzy.appsupport@gmail.com](mailto:wizzy.appsupport@gmail.com)**. We will reply within one month.

If you think we have handled your data incorrectly, you can complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten, IMY) at [imy.se](https://www.imy.se), or to the data protection authority in your own country.

## Children

Wizzy is not intended for children under 16, and you must be at least 16 to create an account. If you believe a child under 16 has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

## Security

Data is sent between the app and our server over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access to each user's data is restricted at the database level, so signed-in users can only read their own data and that of members of their own groups.

## Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above. If the changes are significant, we will let you know in the app.
