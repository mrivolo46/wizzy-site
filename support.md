# Wizzy Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Email **[wizzy.appsupport@gmail.com](mailto:wizzy.appsupport@gmail.com)** and I'll get back to you.

## Common questions

### I'm not getting pings when I walk past landmarks

- Make sure **Pings** is turned on (the bell on the map, or Profile → Settings).
- Allow notifications for Wizzy in the iPhone **Settings** app → Wizzy → Notifications.
- For pings while your phone is locked, set location to **Always** in Settings → Wizzy → Location. With "While Using the App", pings only work while Wizzy is open.
- Pings pause automatically after you've been away from all Wizzy cities for a day. Open Wizzy in a city to turn them back on.

### My city isn't in Wizzy

Wizzy covers a growing list of cities. You can still pick any available city from the city menu to look around. Tell me which city you'd like next.

### How do I join a friend's group?

Open the invite link your friend sent, or go to Profile → Groups → Join and enter the 6-character code.

### I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen, tap **Forgot password** and follow the link in the email. Check your spam folder if it doesn't arrive.

### How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to **Profile → Delete account**. This permanently deletes your account, visits and badges. Groups you created pass to another member. See the [Privacy Policy](../privacy/) for details.

### Does Wizzy track me?

No. Your location stays on your phone, and Wizzy has no ads or analytics. Read the [Privacy Policy](../privacy/).
