Rome · Architecture · 126

Pantheon

Hadrian’s temple, with the largest unreinforced concrete dome ever built.

The inscription names Agrippa, but the standing building is Hadrian’s, around 126 CE. It became a church in 609, which is why it survived.

Look for

Look up at the oculus, open to the sky, then down: small holes in the floor drain the rain that falls through it.

Wander, or plan it. Either way it’s saved.
